Import

Put a move in the game

1. Download move saves move.json.

2. Put it in .minecraft/config/jjk/moves/ (the mod makes the folder; /jjkmove folder shows where).

3. In game: /jjkmove reload, then /jjkmove bind 1 my_move. Keys: 1-5, r, g, q, f.

4. Press the key. /jjkmove play <id> runs it without binding; /jjkmove unbind 1 gives the key back.

Edit an existing animation

Download clip only saves a clip in the mod's own animation format. It goes in src/main/resources/assets/jjk/animations/<group>/; the game picks it up on F3+T or /jjkanim reload.

Controls

Click a body part to pick it, drag it to rotate (Shift: roll, Move tool: shift its position). Drag empty space to orbit, scroll to zoom. Any change at the playhead makes or updates a key. Space plays, arrows step a tick, K keys the whole pose, Delete removes what is selected.

Coordinates: offsets and velocities are right, up, forward from the player, in blocks (velocity: blocks per tick). 1 tick = 50 ms.
